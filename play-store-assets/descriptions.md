🌙 L'enfant do — Your private, 100% offline sleep journal.

Take control of your rest in total serenity. L'enfant do is a sleep tracker built around a simple, uncompromised promise: your health data and sleep routines belong to you and only you.

With strictly zero network permissions, no trackers, and no advertising, L'enfant do provides precise, scientific, and respectful insights into your sleep habits.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🛡️ 100% Offline & Total Privacy
• Zero network permissions: the app cannot connect to the internet, by design.
• All data is stored exclusively on your device.
• No account creation, no sign-up, no hidden telemetry.

🔋 Battery Friendly & Zero Night Drain
• No heavy persistent background services draining your battery while you rest.
• Atomic timestamping when you fall asleep and wake up.
• Minimalist system chronometer notification and Quick Settings tile to track with a single tap.

📊 Mathematically Sound Sleep Analytics
• Circular time mean: eliminates the "midnight trap" where averaging 11:50 PM and 12:10 AM produces noon. Your bedtime and wake-up averages are genuinely accurate.
• Welford variance algorithm for stable sleep regularity and duration tracking.
• Interactive trends and graphs (7d, 14d, 30d, 1y) with smooth monthly aggregation.
• Explicit night coverage rate so missed logs never distort your averages.

🔒 Local Backups & Data Sovereignty
• End-to-end encrypted backup (.enc): secure your sleep history with a password using AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2 key derivation.
• Universal CSV import and export: fully compatible with spreadsheets and legacy Plees Tracker backups.
• Automatic safety snapshots before any restore operation.

🎨 Modern, Soothing & Accessible UI
• Built with Jetpack Compose & Material 3 with a gentle glassmorphism night theme to preserve night vision.
• Optional compact card view for high-density journal browsing.
• Dynamic theming (Dark, Light, System).
